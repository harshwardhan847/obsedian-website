
I actually think you've stumbled onto a content strategy that can be much bigger than just promoting AutoApply.

Looking at your audience:

- **Personal Page (Hindi):** People follow _Harsh_.
    
- **Product Page (English):** People follow _the idea_.

Make it a **"job hunting culture" page.**

Think of it like this:

> **Personal page sells YOU.**
> 
> **Product page sells the PROBLEM.**
> 
> AutoApply just happens to be the solution.

# UGC Ideas That Don't Feel Like Ads

These are the ones I'd prioritize because they look native to Instagram:

### 1. "Spend a day with my AI"

A screen recording throughout the day showing what the AI did while you were working or relaxing.

### 2. "Can AI get me hired?"

Document the experiment over multiple episodes instead of claiming results upfront.

### 3. "Things my AI caught that I would have missed"

Show 3–5 job matches or recruiter details the AI surfaced.

### 4. "Reacting to your job hunting stories"

Read comments from followers and respond with empathy and practical advice. The product appears naturally as something you're building to address those frustrations.

### 5. "Coding a feature requested by a follower"

Take a comment, build it, and show the process.

### 6. "Testing my own AI"

Don't hide flaws. Show when it gets something wrong and how you fix it. That builds credibility.

### 7. "What happened while I slept"

Morning recap:

- Jobs found
    
- High-match opportunities
    
- Emails drafted
    
- Recruiters contacted
    

### 8. "Trying weird job search hacks"

Test viral advice ("Apply at midnight", "Use white text in your resume", etc.), explain what actually works, and mention how you're automating the parts that do.

### 9. "Guess how long this took"

Start with the finished application, let viewers guess, then reveal that most of the process was automated.

### 10. "Building based on comments"

End videos with, "Comment one thing you hate about job hunting." Then make the next video implementing or discussing one of those suggestions.

## One growth loop I think you should build

You already have DM automation, which is a huge advantage. Instead of using it only to deliver the waitlist link, make it feel like the start of a relationship.

Your Reel should end with something like:

> "Comment **HIRE** and I'll send you the exact system I'm building to automate job hunting."

The DM sequence could then be:

1. **Message 1:** Thank them and give them a useful free resource (e.g., a job application checklist or ATS tips).
    
2. **Message 2:** Ask a question: "What's your biggest struggle right now—finding jobs, getting interviews, or tailoring your resume?" This gets replies and boosts engagement.
    
3. **Message 3:** Based on their answer, introduce AutoApply as the tool you're building to solve that exact problem, then invite them to join the waitlist.
    

That sequence feels like help first and promotion second, which is much more likely to build trust and convert followers into enthusiastic early users.
